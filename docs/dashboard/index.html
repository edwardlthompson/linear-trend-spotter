Qualified list

Qualified or watchlist coins from the scanner snapshot. Sort with column header buttons; 7d and 30d columns show gain and hourly-close trend charts—7d uses up to a week of 1h bars, 30d up to a month (white lines = high and low in the window; orange = last close). Star a symbol on the Qualified tab to add it to your watchlist. Use #symbol=BTC (or ?symbol=BTC) to highlight a row. Use the header row (search beside the bell) to filter and Settings to export.
