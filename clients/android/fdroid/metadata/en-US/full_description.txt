Linear Trend Spotter Android companion receives qualified-list change alerts via UnifiedPush with an ntfy-compatible distributor (no Google Play Services or Firebase).

The worker can publish Tier-C ntfy messages when coins enter or leave the qualified list. Subscribe to the operator's topic in the ntfy app, or use this companion when the native Kotlin module is implemented.

FOSS distribution target: F-Droid with reproducible builds (SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH).

Status: scaffold only — Kotlin app under clients/android/app/ is not yet implemented.
